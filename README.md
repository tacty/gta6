SOLMAR HEAT is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game set in Solmar, a fictional coastal city of sunlit streets, beaches, and back alleys. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, weaving through live traffic and crowds of pedestrians as you take on four missions from Lucia. Cause trouble and your wanted level climbs from one to five stars, bringing increasingly aggressive police pursuits down on you. A HUD with a minimap keeps you oriented, your progress is saved in localStorage, and the whole game runs from static files right in your browser. Move with WASD, press E to get in and out of cars, and aim and shoot with the mouse.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # serves game/ at http://localhost:8080
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts   # package the entry into out/
```
